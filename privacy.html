Privacy Policy

Acropolis Web Filter
Last updated: October 2, 2026

Acropolis Web Filter ("Acropolis") is designed to help users filter explicit web content, restrict selected apps and websites, and make impulsive changes to protection settings more difficult. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Acropolis.

Information We Collect

Acropolis does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Acropolis does not require you to create an Acropolis account.

Protection Settings

Acropolis stores information necessary to provide the protection features you configure, such as whether protection is enabled, your selected protection options, unlock settings, and protection timing information.

These settings are used only to provide the functionality you request from Acropolis.

App and Website Selections

Acropolis uses Apple's Family Controls and ManagedSettings technologies to allow you to select and restrict applications, categories, and websites.

Apple provides privacy-preserving representations of selections made through these system frameworks. Acropolis uses these selections to apply the restrictions you request.

Web Filtering

Acropolis provides web-filtering features intended to restrict access to explicit or other blocked web content.

Enhanced Web Filtering may use system-level network or DNS configuration to provide additional filtering. These features are used to determine whether requested web content should be allowed or blocked according to the active protection settings.

Partner PIN

If you use Partner PIN, Acropolis stores the information necessary to verify the PIN and enforce the protection method you selected. The Partner PIN feature is used only to control access to protection settings.

iCloud and CloudKit

Acropolis may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to support protection and recovery functionality. Information stored through these services is handled using Apple's platform services and is used only to provide Acropolis functionality.

Third-Party Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Acropolis does not contain third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

Acropolis does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Acropolis retains app settings and protection information only as needed to provide its features and maintain the protection configuration selected by the user.

Information stored locally may be removed when the applicable settings are reset or when the app and its associated local data are removed, subject to restrictions that may apply while protection is active.

Information stored through Apple services such as iCloud or CloudKit is also subject to Apple's platform controls and data management practices.

Your Choices

You control which Acropolis protection features you configure and which apps or websites you choose to restrict. System permissions used by Acropolis can be managed through the applicable settings provided by iOS, subject to restrictions associated with active protection.

If you have a question about information associated with Acropolis or would like to request assistance with deletion, you may contact us using the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Acropolis does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Acropolis features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
